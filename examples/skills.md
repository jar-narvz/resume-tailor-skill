# Skills inventory (example: Alex Rivera, a made-up person)

Every skill you can speak to in an interview, grouped by category. A
tailored resume may list only items from this file, from `archive.md`'s
`@skills` lines, from a variant's `default_skills` in `profile.yaml`, or a
term named in a bullet on the page. The categories here don't have to match
the labels on the resume.
